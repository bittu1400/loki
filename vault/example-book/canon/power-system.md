# Power System — Driftglass

## What it does

A piece of driftglass replays one echo: a short, fixed fragment of the last
minutes of a person who drowned at that spot. Echoes are not interactive
and do not answer questions. They are evidence, not conversation.

## Rules and limits

- Driftglass forms only where the tide crosses **old stone** (pre-city
  masonry). New harbour works produce nothing.
- An echo replays once per piece, for whoever holds it, then the piece goes
  clear and worthless.
- Iron blinds an echo: glass carried against iron records only the iron.
- There is no known way to create driftglass or to choose whose echo it
  holds.

## Cost

Handling driftglass nightly gives clerks "ledger-ear": they begin hearing
their own name in unrelated sounds. Senior Almanac staff all have it. It is
considered a professional hazard, never discussed.
