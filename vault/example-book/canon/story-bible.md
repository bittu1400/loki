# Story Bible — The Salt Almanac

## Premise

In the harbour city of Vhal Mirek, the tide deposits driftglass: pieces that
carry echoes of those who have drowned. The Almanac Office logs every echo.
When Almanac clerk Ovist Rhoam finds a correction in his own hand that he
never wrote, he discovers someone is editing the city's memory of who went
under — and the ledger of the dead has a hole in it shaped like a person.

## Themes

- Institutions curate grief; curation becomes erasure.
- A record is only as honest as whoever is allowed to amend it.

## Tone

Cold, precise, tidal. Grief handled with clerical detachment until it
cannot be. Dry humour from secondary characters; the POV never jokes first.

## Target length

Novella-scale draft for pipeline testing. Short chapters by design.
