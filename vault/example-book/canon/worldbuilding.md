# Worldbuilding — Vhal Mirek

## The city

Vhal Mirek is built on stacked harbour levels cut into a tidal gorge. The
lowest level, the drowned quarter, floods fully roughly once a generation;
the city treats its schedule as folklore rather than engineering.

## Institutions

- **The Almanac Office** keeps the tide tables and the echo ledger. Its
  clerks log driftglass echoes in a standard small hand; amendments require
  a countersignature that has been "temporarily suspended" for nine years.
- **The divers' benches** are informal guilds along the Rope Walk. They
  price logged glass at fixed rates and unlogged glass at whatever the
  buyer's conscience allows.

## Geography

- The **cut-water** is the office quarter's seaward edge; the Almanac Office
  sits one door in from the Rope Walk stairs.
- The **drowned quarter** lies below the ebb ledge at nine fathoms.

## Economy
