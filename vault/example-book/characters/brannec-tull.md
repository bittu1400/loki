# Bio

Brannec Tull, drift-diver out of the Rope Walk benches. Thirty-ish,
rope-scarred, permanently slightly cold. Works the ebb when sensible divers
stay up, because unlogged glass pays and logged glass doesn't.

## Arc

From man who surfaces with things and tells nobody to man who realises he
is carrying evidence of a second erasure — and that the first one probably
has his own name in it.

## Speech pattern and vocabulary

Short sentences, numbers for weights and depths, nothing wasted. Lies by
omission only, never outright; when cornered he goes quiet rather than
invent.
