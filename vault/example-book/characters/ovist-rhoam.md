# Bio

Ovist Rhoam, mid-fifties, Almanac clerk of the Vhal Mirek Office. Narrow,
precise, salt-grey. Has logged driftglass echoes by lamplight for eleven
years and developed the ledger-ear that comes with it — he hears his own
name in rain on the office roof and has stopped remarking on it.

## Arc

From custodian of a record he trusts absolutely to the only person who can
prove the record has been edited — and having to decide whether an honest
ledger is worth more than the Office that keeps it.

## Speech pattern and vocabulary

Procedural diction used at emotional moments ("I can't file this twice").
Counts things when unsettled. Never uses anyone's first name, including
his own.
