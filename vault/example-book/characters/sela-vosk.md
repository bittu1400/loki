# Bio

Sela Vosk, buyer of unlogged driftglass, niece of the harbourmaster.
Composed, well-dressed for a dockside negotiation, never states what she
actually wants on the first pass.

## Arc

Antagonist-adjacent. Her purchases look like smuggling and resolve into a
private inquest into a specific drowning the Office does not have a page
for.

## Speech pattern and vocabulary

Complete sentences, prices first. Asks questions she already knows the
answer to and waits out the silence until they are answered again,
correctly.
