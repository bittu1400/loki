# Rhythm (hard requirement)

Vary sentence length deliberately. Aim for an average near 14 words, with
real spread: several sentences under 6 words, and at least three that run
past 30 words with subordinate clauses. Do not write long runs of short
declaratives.

Vary how sentences open. No more than two sentences in a row may begin with
the same word, and fewer than one in five sentences overall may begin with
"He" or "She" — use the object, the action, a clause, or a time marker to
open instead.
