# Chapter Summaries — The Salt Almanac

Append-only. One paragraph per chapter, each under a stable `## ch-NNN`
heading so the context builder can slice the last N deterministically.

## ch-001
Ovist Rhoam meets the dusk tide at the Almanac Office steps and logs nine
pieces of driftglass, the tide's usual yield above the cut-water. Sorting
them by weight inside, he finds the spring-tide page already turned: two
corrections stand in the entry, both in his handwriting, neither made by
him. He stands a long time with the page open while a diver's lamp sinks
below the slip.

## ch-002
Brannec Tull dives the falling ebb to nine fathoms against every bench
rule, collecting unlogged glass for Sela Vosk's triple rate. His glove
catches on square-nailed iron holding a lead Office seal to the ebb ledge,
dated this spring — impossible for stone that floods once a generation. He
surfaces telling no one, the lamp-keeper least of all, and means to find
who came down before him with an Office chisel and a reason.
